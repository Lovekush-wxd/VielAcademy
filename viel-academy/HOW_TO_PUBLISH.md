## 🐙 Option 2: GitHub Pages (Free Forever with Unlimited Bandwidth)

1. Go to **[https://github.com](https://github.com)** and log in or create a free account.
2. Click **"New Repository"** and name it `viel-academy`.
3. Click **"uploading an existing file"** and drag these 4 files:
   - `index.html`
   - `styles.css`
   - `three-scene.js`
   - `app.js`
   - And the folder `assets/` (with `logo.png`)
4. Click **"Commit changes"**.
5. In your repository, go to **Settings > Pages**.
6. Under **Branch**, select `main` (or `master`) and click **Save**.
7. 🚀 Your website will be live at:
   `https://<your-github-username>.github.io/viel-academy`

---

## ⚡ Option 3: Vercel (Instant High-Performance Edge)

1. Visit **[https://vercel.com](https://vercel.com)** and sign in.
2. Click **"Add New Project"** > Upload the folder or import your GitHub repository.
3. Click **"Deploy"**.
4. Your website is live in 10 seconds at `https://viel-academy.vercel.app`!
